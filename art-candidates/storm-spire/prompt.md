# Prompts: STORM SPIRE candidate A

Style anchor is the far painting. Scenery and the obstacle sheet were edited from that painting. The wall was edited from the painting, then edited again to strip the painted sky. Generator frames are 1152×864 JPEG. Flat chroma green was keyed to alpha after the fact. Discarded passes are listed so they are not reused.

## storm-background.png

A painterly far background for a top-down vertical arcade shooter, looking straight up the hollow core of a lightning tower. Wide horizontal bands of dark indigo storm cloud press in from the left and right, while the central shaft is a near-black dim column with only faint distant brass pylon specks. Thin amber lightning traces the inner edges of the clouds and stays out of the middle. Low contrast, muted, fully opaque painted sky, quiet enough that bright bullets would read in the center, a pure painting with no lettering.

The delivered PNG is this frame scaled with Lanczos from 1152×864 to 1448×1086.

## storm-scenery.png

A distant cutout layer for the same lightning tower. Only the far left edge and the far right edge hold quiet dark indigo cloud banks and a few slim brass pylon silhouettes with tiny amber sparks. The entire middle of the frame, from well inside the left clouds to well inside the right clouds, is one unbroken field of flat even chroma-key green, solid pure green. No tower, no inset painting, no sky rectangle, and no haze in that green field.

Discarded first edit: the same request with a looser center left a rectangular inset of the far painting in the flight lane.

## storm-wall.png

First edit, from the far painting:

Replace the scene with one single top-down terrain module for a 2D shooter: a dense brass conductor-pylon block with riveted plates, a thick amber lightning conduit, and a heavy readable silhouette in the same indigo and brass palette. Sit the block in the middle with a wide empty margin on every side. That margin is one flat even chroma-key green, solid pure green, with no shadow and no ground.

Second edit, from that result. This is the delivered module:

Keep the same brass and indigo conductor pylon with its amber lightning seam, and remove the painted sky that sits behind it. The metal block is the only object. Every pixel that is not brass plate, indigo steel, bolt, or lightning is flat even chroma-key green, solid pure green, including the backdrop inside the old rectangle. No clouds and no rectangular picture behind the pylon.

## storm-obstacles.png

Replace the scene with a sprite sheet of five separate top-down obstacle modules in one loose row, sharing the indigo, brass, and amber palette of the reference. From left to right: a jagged brass pylon shard, a coil ring with a clear open center, a cloud-anchor weight, a broken vane fin, and a snapped conductor fork. Each piece is a dense flat silhouette. Wide gaps of flat even chroma-key green separate the pieces and fill every corner, solid pure green, with no shadows.
