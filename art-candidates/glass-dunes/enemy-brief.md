# Enemy brief: GLASS DUNES (level 11)

Mixed-orientation level: one concept per orientation. Flat 2D shooter
style, readable silhouettes, dune palette (amber glass, rose dusk,
bleached bone trim). Full-body isolated canonical images, neutral pose,
separated parts, real transparency.

## Concepts

1. `enemy-dune-ripper-a` — Dune ripper. Side-view, facing LEFT. Low
   skiff hull with glass teeth along the prow, sail fin, dust thrusters.
   Visual role: fast horizontal slasher (~112px display). Reference
   behavior: dart — visual context only.
2. `enemy-dune-manta-b` — Glass manta. Top-down view, facing DOWN. Wide
   translucent wingspan, central helm core (weak-point read), trailing
   glass barbs. Visual role: slow vertical bomber (~60px display).
   Reference behavior: riser — visual context only.

Assumptions: 1024×1024 canonical canvases with motion padding; anchors
center. Design artifacts; the playable level reuses the working roster.
