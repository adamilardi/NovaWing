# Prompts: ABYSSAL RELAY candidate A

Style anchor is the far painting. The other three were edited from that image, then the flat blue field was keyed to alpha. Generator aspect was 16:9; the wall edit kept that frame and was cropped to the body plus margin.

## abyssal-background.png

A wide side-view painting of a deep abyssal ocean trench used as the far background of a horizontal 2D arcade shooter. Near-black teal water fills the open center so combat stays quiet and dark, while the trench walls at the far left and right carry faint station lamps in restrained bioluminescent green and rare magenta. Soft distant vent-tower silhouettes sit only at the extreme edges. Painterly, low contrast, muted, opaque, no letters and no interface.

## abyssal-scenery.png

A wide side-view cutout layer of distant abyssal relay silhouettes, using the same dark teal, green, and magenta palette as the reference. A few near-black vent towers and hanging cable strands stand only at the far left and far right, with tiny bioluminescent dots. The entire middle of the frame and every gap around the towers is one flat even chroma-key blue, solid pure blue with no gradient, no haze, and no painted water. Flat orthographic shapes, quiet and dark.

## abyssal-wall.png

One single side-view terrain module for a 2D shooter: a dense vent-chimney block of dark teal rock fused with riveted steel, a short chimney stack, and small bioluminescent green and magenta seams. Flat orthographic arcade sprite, solid readable silhouette, same palette as the reference. The block sits in the middle with a wide empty margin on every side. That margin is one flat even chroma-key blue, solid pure blue, with no shadow and no ground.

## abyssal-obstacles.png

A wide side-view sprite sheet of five separate obstacle modules in a loose row, sharing the reference palette of dark teal rock and steel with small green and magenta bioluminescent edges. From left to right: a jagged vent shard, a coiled cable knot, a broken satellite dish, a bent steel rib, and a cracked relay pod. Each piece is a dense flat orthographic silhouette. Wide gaps of flat even chroma-key blue separate the pieces and fill every corner, solid pure blue with no shadows.

## abyssal-background-1448.png

Extend this same side-view abyssal trench painting into a taller 4:3 frame by continuing the dark teal water above and below. Keep the quiet near-black center open, and keep the same left and right rocky walls, vent-tower silhouettes, and bioluminescent green and magenta edge lamps in the same places and palette. Painterly, low contrast, muted, fully opaque painted water, no letters and no interface.
