# Aurora Passage

Create a cinematic production environment for a new NovaWing level. Camera: top-down. Use existing working enemy art and implementations. Peripheral architecture and a quiet center preserve projectile and ship readability. Original generation is opaque 1448 × 1086 PNG, viewed at 800 × 600 with modest overscan and drifting star parallax. This is distant scenery with no collision geometry. No tiling required; the scene drifts within overscan rather than repeating. Art selection is delegated by the request to create three complete high-quality levels.
